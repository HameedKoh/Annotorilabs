How we work

A workflow with fewer loose ends.

  1. 01

    Intake

    We capture your data source, taxonomy, output format and known edge cases in writing before any work starts.

  2. 02

    Calibration

    Every annotator is tested against a gold standard set and must pass a strict threshold before touching your data.

  3. 03

    Annotation and review

    Work is split into assigned chunks. Each batch goes through self review, sampled review and a full check of flagged items.

  4. 04

    Delivery

    You receive your export in your required format, validated before handoff, with a short report and a summary of edge cases.
